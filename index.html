01 — Introduction

Technical depth, delivered
without the corporate distance.

Kogaso Consultants Limited is a Kenyan firm built around a simple idea: compliance should be practical, not theoretical. We provide Environment and Social Safeguards, Health and Safety, Fire Safety, Agriculture and Livestock services that are technically rigorous, legally sound, and genuinely useful to the people running projects on the ground.

Our team draws from professional bodies, technical institutions, NGOs and government departments — environmentalists, safety and health specialists, a veterinary doctor, animal scientists, agronomists, ecologists, physicians, energy experts, trainers, safety managers and engineers. We are registered under the Companies Act Cap 486 and the Registration of Business Names Act Cap 499.

We work alongside our clients, not above them. That means cost-effective, bespoke solutions that meet statutory requirements (OSHA, EMCA and related statutes) while aligning with international best practice — and a team that is comfortable in a boardroom, a training room, or a construction site at 6 a.m.

04 — Services

Nine services. One team, from
report to site to training room.

Open a service for the detail — including the specific legislation it answers to.

Environmental Impact AssessmentRequired before financing, commencing or carrying out any project. +

Undertaken pursuant to Section 58 of the Environmental Management and Coordination Act, 1999 — a systematic examination to determine whether a programme, activity or project will have adverse impacts on the environment.

EMCA 1999 · §58No proponent may finance, commence, carry out or execute a project before this assessment is complete.
Environmental AuditingPeriodic, objective evaluation of an operating business's compliance. +

Systematic documentation and periodic evaluation of activities and processes to establish whether a business complies with environmental requirements and enforcing legislation — testing the effectiveness of environmental efforts against the business's own environmental management plan.

EMCA 1999 · §68Conducted under the Environmental (Impact Assessment and Audit) Regulations.
Resettlement Action PlansMitigating physical and economic displacement from large-scale projects. +

We develop resettlement action plans (RAP) and livelihood restoration plans that address both physical displacement — loss of home and community — and economic displacement, including loss of assets, access to assets, or livelihood, caused by large-scale infrastructure and development projects.

Risk AssessmentsWhere risk management begins — for people, and for equipment. +

We evaluate hazards to determine whether an exposed population faces greater-than-expected risk of injury or ill health, or whether equipment and machinery are at risk of damage that leads to lost production. It's the entry point for the whole risk management process, giving employers a clear view of what's needed to improve workplace health, safety and productivity.

EHS Site Supervision — ConstructionA site officer on the ground for the life of the contract. +

Full OSH supervision and inspection for clients undertaking construction, delivered by our site officer. This covers:

  • Environmental and Social Management Plans, plus site audits and inspections
  • Safety and health specifications for the project
  • EHS plans compiled on behalf of the client
  • Evaluation and approval of the principal contractor's EHS plans
  • Monitoring the principal contractor's EHS compliance for the contract period
  • Statutory audits and inspections, with follow-up on recommendations
  • Attendance at site EHS meetings to confirm contractor compliance
  • Periodic EHS audit reports and performance-tracking measurements
Occupational Noise Survey & MappingAnnual measurement wherever noise sources are present. +

Noise measurement is required at least once every twelve months in every workplace with noise emission sources. We survey occupational and environmental noise, covering diurnal and nocturnal schedules and the most noise-sensitive locations nearby — residential premises, schools, hospitals, places of worship, parks — using an integrating sound level meter, and recommend practical, economically viable ways to eliminate the hazard.

MethodologyMeasurement follows the international standard ISO 1996.
Training & AwarenessOSH committees, first aid and fire safety — as the law requires. +

OSH Committee Training — Section 9 of the Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2007 requires every occupier of a workplace with twenty or more employees to establish a safety and health committee. Legal Notice No. 31 of 2004 then requires every committee member to complete basic OSH training within six months of appointment, and refresher training thereafter.

First Aid Training — required under Legal Notice No. 160, the Factories (First Aid) Rules, 1977, for anyone responsible for a first aid box, taught through a practical approach centred on the specific risks employees actually face.

Basic Fire Safety Training — required under the Factories and Other Places of Work (Fire Risk Reduction) Rules, 2007, covering:

  1. Fire losses & regulations
  2. Duties of employers & fire marshals
  3. The science of fire
  4. The fire triangle
  5. Workplace fire hazards
  6. Classes of fire
  7. Principles of extinction
  8. Extinguisher types & codes
  9. Choosing rescue equipment
  10. Fire risk mapping
  11. Fire prevention
  12. Emergency preparedness
  13. Evacuation procedures
  14. Practical firefighting
  15. Managing fire emergencies
AgricultureEnterprise and project design across the food system. +

We work across the different aspects of food and farming systems — enterprise and product development, project design and management, communication and advocacy, among others.

LivestockAnimal health, welfare and value-chain development. +
